Tools

Four tools, one address each.

Source verified: all of these are exact matches on Sourcify — the code you can read is the bytecode that is deployed. Check the MultiSender as an example, or paste any address from this page into Sourcify or a block explorer.

Everything here is a contract at the same address on every chain we deploy to — the same bytecode, verifiable on the explorer, with no owner behind any of it. Your wallet's chain decides which one you are talking to. No protocol fee on any tool — you pay gas only.

01

Multi-sender

Pay or distribute to up to 400 addresses in one transaction. Native currency or any ERC-20; the even split gives the division remainder back rather than keeping it.

 02

Token

Deploy a fixed-supply ERC-20 in one transaction. No owner, no mint, no pause, no burn — the whole supply goes to whoever signs, and nothing can create more.

 03

Airdrop

Publish a list as a Merkle root and let people claim their own share. The list never goes on chain; the root is the commitment, and anyone can submit a claim for someone else.

 04

Launchpad

Run a sale: a fixed price, a window, a soft cap that decides whether it happens at all. Miss the soft cap and every contribution goes back — the contract has no way to keep it.
