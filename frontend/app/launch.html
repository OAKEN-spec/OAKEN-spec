Third-party routes are not ownerless. Pons, Flap, Genius, Clanker and four.meme run their own contracts and backends. Their teams can change factories, fees, hooks or rules — Clanker's factory owner can change hooks and modules; four.meme signs launches from its backend; Flap's portal is upgradeable by its team. Only our own factory is fully ownerless. The warning for the selected route is shown in its panel — read it before you sign.
