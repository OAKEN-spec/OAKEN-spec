Solana · launch

Two doors onto Solana.

OAKEN Factory writes a fixed-supply SPL token with no mint authority and no owner. pump.fun puts it on its bonding curve. Your wallet signs both; OAKEN takes no fee. A third door — OAKEN Curve — is built and tested but not on mainnet yet; its status is listed below rather than hidden.

2 · Choose a route

OAKEN Factory Fixed-supply SPL token + Metaplex metadata. The second transaction revokes the mint authority — the result panel tells you if that step landed. No owner, no pause, no OAKEN fee. There is no market until you create one.
pump.fun Create the coin on pump.fun's bonding curve. pump.fun's program and rules run the market, graduation and fees; the creator share is paid by pump.fun, not by us.
OAKEN Curve Not deployed A 1B-supply bonding curve on OAKEN's own Solana program. Buys and sells go through the program; graduation puts the remaining tokens and the raise into a permissionless PumpSwap pool whose LP cannot be moved. The program is built and tested, but it is not on mainnet yet.
OAKEN Factory creates the token directly; pump.fun is a third-party launch service.

    Reading the chain…
