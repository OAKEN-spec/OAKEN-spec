WHAT IS NOT HERE

No owner. No upgrade. No audit.

Anything an operator can do is something you eventually have to trust them about. So none of it was built. The vault is a dead end by construction — there is no function for anyone to call.

no audit 0

Audits claimed

We do not claim one. The contract is short and public — read it instead of trusting a badge.

Why no owner?

An owner key is a promise that someone can change the rules later. We removed the key instead of asking you to trust the promise.

  • No upgrade path. The contract holding your tokens cannot be replaced, patched or pointed elsewhere.
  • No rescue function. That cuts both ways: we cannot recover a mistake, and nobody else can either.
  • Nothing to renounce. There is no key to renounce, because none was kept.

THE SCHEDULE, DRAWN

Vesting is a set of rings. Each one is a date.

A stepped lock releases a slice every N days — say 10% at TGE, then 5% a month, eighteen times. The record page draws exactly that: one ring per release, filling as the chain says it filled. No chart of promises — a calendar you can count.

THE LINE WE DO NOT BLUR

Locks are ownerless. Bonds are not.

Do not read “no owner” and assume it applies to every product. Locks and Bonds have different trust assumptions, and every page says which is which.

Locks · vault, positions, tools

  • No admin, no pause, no upgrade. The schedule is fixed once the lock lands.
  • Anyone can pay the gas to release a due lock; the vault pays only the beneficiary.
  • No protocol fee. You pay gas only.

Bonds · aOAKEN

  • Admin = 2-of-3 Safe + 24h Timelock. Parameters can change; that is a disclosed governance surface.
  • Reserves are not the admin's to move. Redemption depends on USDG reserves and can be limited by them.
  • No external yield promise. OAKEN emissions are emissions, not a market yield; there is no aOAKEN market here.
