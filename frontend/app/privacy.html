Privacy

The short version: no accounts, no analytics.

Summary

OAKEN does not create accounts, does not run analytics or advertising scripts, and does not sell data. Your wallet address and the contents of your transactions are public on the chain. The site stores a few preferences in your browser and sends chain reads to public RPC providers. That is the whole picture; the details are below.

What you provide

  • Wallet address. Read from your wallet when you connect. It is used to build the page (your locks, balances, positions) and is never sent to us as an account.
  • Transactions. Everything you sign is public on the chain. We cannot delete it and neither can you.
  • Images you upload. Artwork for our own factory is stored on an off-chain host and is publicly retrievable by URL. Do not upload anything private.
  • Messages to us. If you contact us on X, that conversation is handled by X under its own privacy policy.

What your browser sends

  • RPC requests. Chain reads go from your browser to public RPC providers. They see your IP address and the addresses you query, like any block explorer does. You can avoid this by using a wallet or node you control.
  • Hosting logs. The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes standard request logs (IP, user agent, path) to deliver the site and protect it.
  • localStorage. Preferences such as the selected chain, the boot animation flag and the last-used wallet type. They stay in your browser; clearing site data removes them.
  • No cookies for tracking. The site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Third-party services you open (explorers, X, launchpads) set their own.

Third-party services

Depending on what you use, the site may contact:

  • · Public RPC providers listed in the chain registry.
  • · Cloudflare Pages (hosting) and our image host for uploaded artwork.
  • · WalletConnect's relay, only if you choose WalletConnect from the connect panel.
  • · Block explorers and Sourcify, when you click a verification link.
  • · Third-party launchpads (Pons, Flap, Genius, Clanker, four.meme) when you choose their route. Their terms and privacy practices are theirs.

Retention and your choices

  • No account data to delete. There is no account database to delete from.
  • Clear your browser. Clearing site data removes localStorage preferences. Disconnecting the wallet ends the page's access to your address.
  • Uploaded images remain publicly retrievable while the image host keeps them. We do not promise permanent storage.
  • Children. The service is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone below the legal age in their jurisdiction.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the current version will be on this page.
