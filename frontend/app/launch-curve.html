How this launch works

1 · The curve. All supply starts in the curve. Buyers pay the chain's native coin; the price follows a constant product. There is no team allocation, so there is nothing to dump at open.
2 · The line. When the sellable allocation is gone, the curve is complete. The last buy is capped and the unused coin is refunded.
3 · Graduation. Anyone can call it. The remaining tokens and the raise go into a DEX position owned by a contract with no withdraw function. The pool is seeded once.
4 · Then what. The token trades on the DEX. On a tax token, buys and sells pay the configured tax; on a standard token there is no tax.
The creator earns trade fees (and the optional creator tax), not a token allocation. Buying at launch is buying from the same curve as everyone else.
