1 · What OAKEN is

OAKEN is a set of public smart contracts and a website that reads them. No operator can move a lock, pause it or take a fee. RISK DISCLOSURE

What can go wrong.

  • Smart-contract risk. A bug, an unexpected token behaviour or a chain-level failure can cause a transaction to revert or behave differently from the description. The contracts are short and public, but they are not guaranteed to be free of defects.
  • Irreversibility. Blockchains do not reverse transactions. A lock, a launch, a transfer or a renounce is final. We cannot recover tokens sent to the wrong address, locked with the wrong beneficiary, or locked into a schedule you later regret.
  • Token risk. Fee-on-transfer, rebase, blacklist, pause and max-transaction tokens can make a lock hold a different amount than expected, or block a transfer entirely. Exclude the adapter and the vault from fees and limits before locking. Do not lock rebase tokens here.
  • Market and liquidity risk. A lock does not make a token valuable or liquid. Prices can go to zero. A locked position can be worth less than when it was locked.
  • Third-party risk. Pons, Flap, Genius, Clanker and four.meme are independent services with their own contracts, backends and upgrade rights. Their rules, fees and hooks can change. A third-party launch is not covered by OAKEN's lock model.
  • Upgrade risk (v4). A 2-of-3 multisig can replace the v4 vault implementation after a 48-hour delay. That can fix a bug, but it can also change behaviour, and a compromised quorum could upgrade to code you did not expect. The delay is visible on-chain and an upgrade can be cancelled before it executes. Locks in v1–v3 cannot be upgraded.
  • Bonds risk. aOAKEN is a separate product with an admin (2-of-3 Safe + 48h Timelock). Redemptions depend on vault reserves and can be limited by them. There is no external yield promise, no aOAKEN market on this site, and OAKEN emissions are not a dollar yield.
  • Off-chain metadata. Token images for our own factory are stored off-chain. If that host is unavailable, the image may disappear while the token remains on chain.
  • Regulatory risk. The legal treatment of tokens, locks and sales varies by jurisdiction and can change. You are responsible for your own compliance.
