NEW PROPOSAL

Where the rewards come from. Stream 0 is USDG: the 10 bps redemption fee this bond's vault charges, paid straight into the staking pool. Stream 1 is OAKEN: a budget the treasury transfers in. Both are finite and visible on-chain — the pool can only ever pay out what it holds. Rates above are annualised from the currently funded budget and the current pool size; when a budget runs out the rate goes to zero, and nothing here is a promise of return. OAKEN is an incentive token, not a dollar yield.

Lock it. aOAKEN is an ERC-20. Use Oaken Lock to vest or lock it — paste the bond address from the Contracts block below.

Reward sources. 100,000 OAKEN emissions (funded 2026-10-17), a 0.01 USDG treasury bootstrap, and the 0.10% redemption fee stream. Every funding transaction is in the history table above. No external yield is promised.

NAV. The oracle currently publishes 1.00000000 USDG. No coupon is being funded yet; the vault holds only USDG, and redemptions are limited by its reserves. Nothing here promises a yield.

What backs it. aOAK is minted against USDG at NAV 1.00. The vault holds only USDG; the only USDG exit is redeem. No stock or sovereign-bond instrument is tokenized on this page.

Admin. The vault, NAV oracle and staking pool are administered by a 2-of-3 Safe 0x5E8C4c241a29ff7E60c648f378A4AA70053Ee92e, not a single key. The Safe can update params, but cannot move a staked bond or a vault reserve. The NAV keeper is a separate hot key; NAV and parameter changes are queued through the 24h Timelock, and anyone can execute them once the delay has passed.
