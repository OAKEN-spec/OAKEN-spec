New lock

Put it where it cannot be moved.

Five steps: connect the wallet, paste the token address, pick how much to lock (Lock all fills in your whole balance), approve it — one approval covers every later lock of that token — then lock. The schedule is fixed the moment it lands; nobody, including us, can move it afterwards.

Not sure about the schedule? Run a pre-launch review with the OAKEN agent →

Details

New locks go to the v4 vault. A 2-of-3 multisig can replace the vault code, but only after a public 48-hour delay; no key can move a lock's tokens or change its schedule directly. v1–v3 locks are immutable. Upgrade model →
The lock lands on the chain your wallet is on. Pick another one here and we will ask the wallet to switch before signing. Solana opens its own lock page — it uses a Solana wallet.
