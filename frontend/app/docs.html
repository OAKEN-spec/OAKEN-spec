Docs

The short version, in one page.

OAKEN is a lock service and a small launchpad. On the EVM chains we deploy to, tokens and liquidity go into contracts that have no owner, no upgrade path and no admin key — nothing to renounce, because nothing was kept. Solana locks are different: they currently use Jupiter Lock, a third-party, upgradeable program — the Solana section below says exactly what is live. Everything this site shows is read from the chain it sits on; the site is a window, not the vault. Audit and bug-bounty status, coverage and known limitations are on the Security page — no audit claim is made.

Solana — two launch routes, one lock route

OAKEN Factory (own launch) creates a classic SPL mint with a fixed supply, writes Metaplex metadata, and then revokes the mint authority. There is no OAKEN program in this path: the token is governed by the SPL Token and Metaplex programs, both live on Solana. You pay rent and network fees only.

pump.fun is a third-party route: its program creates the coin on its bonding curve. pump.fun's market, graduation and fee rules apply, and its program is upgradeable by pump.fun.

Locks on Solana currently use Jupiter Lock (LocpQgucEQHbqNABEYvBvwoxCPsSbG91A1QaQhQQqjn), a third-party vesting program. It is upgradeable by Jupiter's team. OAKEN's own Solana lock program (Co9ZnAVxX2siGThhjTpPgXPHYrCev1nrXFyGB1VcZcQW) is built and tested but not deployed to mainnet; the Solana locks page says so before it asks for a signature. EVM locks on Robinhood Chain and BNB Chain are live and unchanged.

Solana wallets are separate from the EVM wallet in the top bar. The Solana pages detect Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and Glow in the browser; nothing is signed until you approve it in that wallet.

HOW TO CHECK A LOCK YOURSELF

Three ways, none of them through us.

01

Open its record page

Every number there is an eth_call from your browser to a public node — not a value we stored. If the page and a block explorer ever disagree, believe the explorer.

02

Call the vault directly

Paste 0xe410d0a2… into a block explorer, open getLock(id) and hit read. It returns the same amounts, dates and steps this site shows.

03

Run your own node

Nothing here is required for a lock to release on time. The schedule lives in the contract; when it is due, the vault pays the beneficiary and nobody else.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

What this does not do.

  • No rebase tokens. A balance that changes on its own breaks the arithmetic of "how much is in there" — the lock would be wrong the moment it lands.
  • Fee-on-transfer and max-tx tokens need care. Exclude the adapter 0x16e02A10… and the vault 0xe410d0a2… from fees, limits and blacklists first. The vault measures what actually arrived, so a taxed transfer locks less than you sent — the record page will say so.
  • A renounced lock is permanent. There is no un-renounce — that is the point — so it is offered only when the schedule is already what you want.
  • Position locks hold ERC-721s. Their strength is the NFT's own: a collection with a back door can move the position anyway. The collection address is part of every record, and "this LP is locked" is only ever said about known position managers.
  • Token images are off-chain. For our own factory the chain has no image field; the artwork is stored on our image host. If that host is unavailable, the token still exists and trades — only the picture is missing. Third-party launches use their own metadata.
  • We cannot recover mistakes. Tokens sent to the wrong address, or locked with no beneficiary, are gone. Nobody — including us — has a rescue function, because nothing was kept.
  • This is new. The contracts are short and public, but young. Start with an amount you are comfortable testing.
FAQ

The questions the form does not answer.

Which wallets work?

EVM: any browser wallet that injects a standard provider (EIP-1193 / EIP-6963) — MetaMask, OKX, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Rainbow and friends — or WalletConnect from the connect panel. Solana pages detect Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and Glow separately; the EVM wallet in the top bar is not used there. The site never asks for a seed phrase.

What does it cost?

Locks cost nothing. You pay gas, that is all. Third-party launchpads (Pons, Flap, Clanker, four.meme) charge their own fees — shown before you sign — and those go to them, not to us.

Where does the money go?

Nowhere. The vault holds tokens and pays the beneficiary, and there is no other function to call. OAKEN has no treasury in any of these contracts.

Who can claim a lock?

Only the beneficiary. Anyone may pay the gas to send it to them — the vault checks who is being paid, not who is paying.

What if this website disappears?

Locks still release. The schedule is in the contract, and the contract is on four chains. This site is a reader; it is not required for any of it.

Why is the launchpad listing sometimes empty?

Sales come from a listing snapshot plus live clocks. When nothing is inside its window the list says so, and when the listing itself cannot be reached the page says that instead of pretending there are no sales.

Can I lock on Solana?

Yes — with Jupiter Lock, a third-party vesting program, while OAKEN's own Solana lock program is not yet deployed to mainnet. The Solana locks page states which program will be used and that Jupiter's program is upgradeable before it asks for a signature. EVM locks on Robinhood Chain and BNB Chain are our own contracts.

Something is stuck on “Reading…”

Chain reads go straight from your browser to public nodes. If a node stalls, the page now gives up after a few seconds and offers Retry — which also rotates to the chain's second RPC. Nothing you sign is affected by a read failing.

A transaction failed. What now?

Nothing was sent, unless the wallet says otherwise — a revert costs only the gas of the attempt, and your tokens stay where they were. The page shows the reason in plain words: a rejection in the wallet, not enough native currency for gas, a token that refuses transfers, or a number the contract will not take. The usual fixes are “approve first”, “top up the gas balance”, or “raise the release % per cycle”.

My wallet is on another chain.

Pick the chain you want in the header — the site asks your wallet to switch, and offers to add the chain if the wallet does not have it yet. If your wallet sits on a chain we have not deployed to, the header says Wrong network and asks once per session; nothing is ever sent there.

What is “amount” on a record page?

What the vault is actually holding for that lock — measured when the transfer landed, not what the sender typed. For a fee-on-transfer token the two differ, and the record page says so.
