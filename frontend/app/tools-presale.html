If it fails — self-check

  • Fund the whole hard cap first. The sale contract must hold enough tokens for the hard cap before it opens; the factory refuses a sale it cannot back.
  • The window is chain time. A start time already in the past reverts. The form checks it against the chain, not your computer clock.
  • Miss the soft cap → refunds. Every buyer can take their contribution back; the creator cannot keep it. The raise is not released to the creator until the sale succeeds.
  • Finalize is permissionless. After the window and a met soft cap, anyone can push it over the line; buyers can claim as soon as it is final.
